When the guest list grows, the food should still taste like home. We cook for house parties, poojas, birthdays, office lunches and weddings, and we take bulk biryani orders too.

## What food can we order for catering?

We cook biryanis, pulaos, kebabs and Telugu specials for catering, and take bulk biryani orders.

- Biryanis and pulaos
- Kebabs
- Telugu specials from both Andhra Pradesh and Telangana
- Bulk biryani orders for larger gatherings

Veg and non-veg are both on the menu, from mild to proper Andhra hot. Tell us who is coming and we will help you pick.

## Occasions we cook for

- Poojas
- Birthdays
- Office lunches
- House parties
- Weddings
