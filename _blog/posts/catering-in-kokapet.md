<h2>What can you order for catering?</h2>
<p>Our catering choices include biryanis, pulaos, kebabs and Telugu specials from Andhra Pradesh and Telangana. We also take bulk biryani orders. Both vegetarian and non-vegetarian choices are available; we’ll confirm the dishes for your date and quantity when preparing your quote.</p>

<h2>Build the meal around your guests</h2>
<p>A good place to start is the main course. Would your guests enjoy biryani, pulao, or a Telugu meal with curries? Once you have that starting point, discuss starters and accompaniments with us. A shorter selection that suits your group can make planning easier than choosing several dishes before considering quantities.</p>
<p>For an office meal, share the serving time, vegetarian and non-vegetarian guest counts, and any delivery or building-access instructions. For a house party or birthday, tell us whether food will be the main meal or part of a longer gathering. These details help us discuss quantities around the way you plan to serve.</p>
<p>For a pooja or vegetarian gathering, make your food and preparation requirements clear at the start. We operate a mixed kitchen serving vegetarian and non-vegetarian food, so please discuss any specific restrictions with our team before confirming.</p>
<p>Tell us whether your group prefers mild, medium or spicy food. We’ll discuss what is possible for your chosen dishes. If children and adults have different preferences, mention that too, rather than assuming every dish can be adjusted in the same way.</p>
<h2>How much notice do we need?</h2>
<p>We accept catering orders <strong>one day before the event</strong>, but <strong>three days in advance is preferred</strong>. The extra time lets us discuss your menu, quantity and arrangements before the event day.</p>
<p>When you contact us, share both the event date and the time you want to serve the food. For a next-day order, send those details along with your guest count and preferred dishes so we can confirm the arrangements. Your enquiry becomes a confirmed order only after you and our team have agreed the details.</p>
<h2>How are catering prices and quantities decided?</h2>
<p>Our catering menu and charges depend on the <strong>quantity ordered</strong>. We’ll prepare a quote around the food and arrangements discussed for your event; there is no fixed package or per-person price listed in this guide.</p>
<p>Along with the total guest count, tell us how many people want vegetarian or non-vegetarian food and whether you are serving other food at the event. For a bulk biryani order, ask us to recommend the quantity for your group rather than estimating portions from a restaurant menu size label.</p>
<p>Before confirming, review the dish names, quantities, total price, serving or delivery time and any agreed services with us. If your plans change, contact us to discuss the revised order and quote.</p>
<h2>Delivery, buffet setup, equipment and staff</h2>
<p>Delivery, setup, serving equipment and serving staff <strong>can be discussed for each order</strong>. They are not automatically included with every catering enquiry. Tell us whether you need food delivered, help arranging the serving area, equipment or people to serve your guests.</p>
<p>We discuss catering requests across Hyderabad from our Kokapet restaurant. Share the exact event location so we can confirm delivery availability and any location-related charges. If your venue has a delivery slot, lift restrictions or a designated setup area, include those details when asking for a quote.</p>
