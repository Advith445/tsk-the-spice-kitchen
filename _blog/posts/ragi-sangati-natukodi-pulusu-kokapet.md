<h2 id="understand-the-dish">What are ragi sangati and natukodi pulusu?</h2>
<p><strong>Ragi sangati</strong> is a cooked finger-millet preparation shaped into portions and eaten with a gravy. Ragi means finger millet. In TSK's combination, the sangati contains both finger millet and rice.</p>
<p>The <a href="https://ananthapuramu.ap.gov.in/culinary-delight/ragi-mudda-ragi-sangati/">Ananthapuramu district guide</a> identifies ragi sangati, also known as ragi mudda, with Rayalaseema food. Recipes vary, so a regional description does not establish every restaurant's ingredients.</p>
<p><strong>Natukodi pulusu</strong> is country chicken in a sour gravy, commonly made with tamarind. You may also see the name written as naatu kodi or naatukodi. Telugu recipe writer <a href="https://vismaifood.com/en/naatukodi-pulusu-country-chicken-sour-curry">Vismai Food describes this curry and its pairing with ragi sangati</a>.</p>
<p>The two parts are eaten together: sangati provides a soft base, and pulusu adds gravy and chicken. For other regional dishes on our menu, explore <a href="https://tskthespicekitchenkokapet.in/blog/seven-signature-plates-and-where-they-come-from/">TSK's signature plates from Andhra Pradesh and Telangana</a>.</p>

<h2 id="how-to-eat">How do you eat ragi sangati with natukodi pulusu?</h2>
<p>Take a small piece of sangati, combine it with some pulusu and eat the chicken alongside it. A spoon works well if you prefer one.</p>
<ol><li>Allow the food to cool to a comfortable temperature.</li><li>Take a small piece of sangati with your fingers or a spoon.</li><li>Dip it into the pulusu or add a little gravy to your spoon.</li><li>Eat the two together, then add chicken and more gravy as you prefer.</li></ol>
<p>Our menu describes the curry as fiery. Start with a little gravy to judge the chilli level, and tell the team about your spice preference before ordering. Any adjustment depends on the available preparation options.</p>
