Every dish on our Signatures list comes from a particular corner of Andhra Pradesh or Telangana. Here are the seven plates our guests come back for, and the place each one is from.

## Hyderabad: Chicken Dum Biryani

Long-grain basmati layered over marinated chicken, sealed and slow-cooked on dum. We serve it with mirchi ka salan and raita, the way Hyderabad eats it.

![Chicken dum biryani in a steel handi with a leg piece and egg, with raita and salan](/blog/media/biryani.webp)

## Konaseema: Kodi Chips

Thin strips of chicken fried crisp and tossed with curry leaves, green chillies and roasted cashews. A favourite from the coconut country of the Godavari delta.

![Konaseema kodi chips on a black tray with cashews and a carved carrot flower](/blog/media/kodi.webp)

## Rayalaseema: Ragi Sangati with Naatukodi Pulusu

Soft balls of finger millet and rice with a fiery country-chicken curry. Break the sangati with your fingers and dip it in the pulusu.

Our [ragi sangati with natukodi pulusu guide](/blog/ragi-sangati-natukodi-pulusu-kokapet/) explains the pairing and what to ask before ordering.

![Two ragi sangati balls beside a bowl of naatukodi pulusu](/blog/media/ragi.webp)

## Karimnagar: Fried Wings

Chicken wings in a Telangana red-chilli marinade, fried until the edges crackle.

## Telangana: Golinchina Mushroom

Golinchina is how Telangana pan-roasts meat with onion, chilli and garam masala. We cook mushrooms the same way, for vegetarians who like heat.

![Golinchina mushroom on a black plate with onion and lemon](/blog/media/mushroom.webp)

## Kokapet: Paneer Kurkure

Paneer in a crunchy, spiced coating, fried golden outside and soft within. A favourite with our vegetarian guests.

## Hyderabad: Apricot Delight

Stewed apricot under a layer of silky cream, topped with chopped nuts and served chilled. It is the sweet way to finish a Telugu meal, in a small cup or a large one to share.

![Apricot delight in a white cup on a black plate, topped with cream and chopped nuts](/blog/media/apricot.webp)

## How hot will it be?

Andhra food is famously hot. Tell your server, or mention it in your order, and the kitchen will make your dish mild, medium or proper Andhra hot.
