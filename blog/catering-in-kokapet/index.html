Catering from TSK Kokapet: what we cook and how to ask

House parties, poojas, birthdays, office lunches and weddings. What TSK cooks for celebrations and how to ask for a catering quote.

When the guest list grows, the food should still taste like home. We cook for house parties, poojas, birthdays, office lunches and weddings, and we take bulk biryani orders too.

What we cook

  • Biryanis and pulaos
  • Kebabs
  • Telugu specials from both Andhra Pradesh and Telangana
  • Bulk biryani orders for larger gatherings

Veg and non-veg are both on the menu, from mild to proper Andhra hot. Tell us who is coming and we will help you pick.

Occasions we cook for

  • Poojas
  • Birthdays
  • Office lunches
  • House parties
  • Weddings

How to ask for a quote

Message us on WhatsApp at +91 93986 93051 with the date, the number of guests and the kind of food you have in mind, and our team will reply with a quote.

Bringing a group to TSK instead

Our garden room is a private dining room with tables for groups, and a screen on request. For 10 or more guests, please call us on +91 99490 51777 rather than booking online.
