A closer look at fry-piece and boneless biryani

If you enjoy fried chicken pieces with rice, start with Chicken Fry Piece Biryani. If you prefer chicken without bones, choose TSK Boneless Biryani. For details about a particular preparation or the sides included with your order, ask our team.

Regular or family: how much biryani should you order?

A regular biryani serves one person, and a family pack serves two to three people. All three chicken biryanis have both options. Use this as a starting point; appetites and the other dishes you order will affect how much your table needs.

For one person, start with a regular portion. For two or three people sharing, consider a family pack alongside any starters or other dishes you want. If biryani is the main meal for everyone, tell us your guest count so we can help you plan quantities.

Check the selected portion and price in the biryani menu or your chosen delivery app before paying. For a larger gathering, use our catering guide to plan your enquiry.

Before You Order Chicken Biryani at TSK

Which chicken biryani at TSK is boneless?

TSK Boneless Biryani is our house biryani with boneless chicken. Choose it if you prefer chicken without bones.

How is Chicken Fry Piece Biryani different from Chicken Dum Biryani?

Chicken Fry Piece Biryani has biryani rice layered with spicy fried chicken pieces. For Chicken Dum Biryani, basmati is layered over marinated bone-in chicken, sealed and slow-cooked on dum.

Does Chicken Dum Biryani come with raita and salan?

Yes. We list raita and salan with Chicken Dum Biryani. Check the item details on your chosen delivery platform when placing an order.

Can I ask for less spicy biryani?

Tell us your preference before ordering. We will discuss suitable options and confirm which adjustments are possible for your chosen dish.

How many people does a family biryani serve?

A family pack serves two to three people, and a regular portion serves one person. All three chicken biryanis have both options. Appetites and the rest of your meal will affect the quantity you need.

Can I order biryani for a party or catering event?

Yes. We take bulk biryani orders and catering enquiries for groups of 8 or more people across Hyderabad. Share your event date, location, guest count and food preferences. Our team will confirm available dishes, quantities and a quote. Read our catering guide or call +91 99490 51777 to discuss your order.
