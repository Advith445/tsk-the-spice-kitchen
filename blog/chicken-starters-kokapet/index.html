Chicken starters in Kokapet: Telugu fries, majestic or kebabs?

Choose a chicken starter at TSK in Kokapet: Telugu fries like Konaseema Kodi Chips, Hyderabad's Chicken Majestic, or kebabs from our tandoor.

Choosing a chicken starter in Kokapet? At TSK The Spice Kitchen, order Konaseema Kodi Chips or Karimnagar Fried Wings for crisp Telugu fries, Chicken Majestic for a Hyderabad restaurant classic, or Chicken Malai Kebab if you want something from the tandoor with mild spices.

We serve all of these at our restaurant on Gandipet Main Road, Kokapet, Hyderabad. This guide sorts our chicken starters into three styles, then covers bones, spice and how much to order.

Which chicken starter should you choose?

Start with the style your table enjoys. Our menu groups chicken starters three ways: Telugu fries, Indo-Chinese favourites and kebabs from the tandoor.

Telugu fries: kodi chips, wings and vepudu

These come from the Non-veg starters on our menu. Kodi means chicken in Telugu, and vepudu means fry.

  • Konaseema Kodi Chips: thin strips of chicken fried crisp with curry leaves, green chillies and cashews. Konaseema is the coconut country of the Godavari delta in Andhra Pradesh.
  • Karimnagar Fried Wings: chicken wings in a Telangana red-chilli marinade, fried until the edges crackle.
  • Golinchina Chicken: golinchina is the Telangana way of pan-roasting meat with onion, chilli and garam masala.

The same section also lists three vepudus: Natu Kodi Vepudu, made with country chicken (natu kodi); Pachi Mirchi Kodi Vepudu, named for green chilli (pachi mirchi); and TSK Kaju Kodi Vepudu, named for cashews (kaju). Browse them all in our non-veg starters.

Indo-Chinese favourites: majestic, 65 and manchuria

  • Chicken Majestic: strips of fried chicken tossed with yoghurt, green chillies and curry leaves. Our menu calls it a Hyderabad restaurant classic.
  • Chicken Manchuria: fried chicken tossed in a glossy Manchurian sauce with garlic and spring onion.
  • Chicken 65, Chicken Chilli and Chicken Lollipops are in the same section, along with Dragon Chicken and Pepper Chicken.

From the tandoor: kebabs

Our kebabs are marinated overnight and roasted in our tandoor, the clay oven.

  • Chicken Malai Kebab: chicken marinated in cream and mild spices.
  • Tandoor Chicken: chicken on the bone in a red tandoori marinade, charred in the clay oven.
  • Tangdi Kebab: chicken drumsticks marinated in yoghurt and spices, then roasted in the tandoor.

The kebab menu also lists Chicken Tikka, Pahadi Chicken Kebab and Barwan Tangdi Kebab.

Bone-in or boneless?

If bones matter to you, the menu descriptions help. Karimnagar Fried Wings, Tandoor Chicken, Tangdi Kebab and Chicken Drum Sticks are on the bone. Konaseema Kodi Chips and Chicken Majestic are made with strips of chicken.

For any other starter, ask us before ordering, and we will tell you how it is cut.

Which chicken starter is less spicy?

Our menu describes Chicken Malai Kebab as marinated in cream and mild spices, so it is a good place to start for someone who prefers milder food. Karimnagar Fried Wings use a red-chilli marinade, so ask about the heat if you are unsure.

Tell us how spicy you like your food before ordering. We will help you choose a dish and confirm which adjustments are possible.

How many chicken starters should you order?

There is no fixed number. It depends on how many people are sharing and whether a main course follows. If biryani or curries are coming next, a simple plan is to start with two starters in different styles, such as a Telugu fry and a kebab, and add more if you need them.

Tandoor Chicken and Tangdi Kebab come in half and full portions. For a larger group, tell us how many people are eating and what else you are ordering, and we will help you plan.

Planning the main course too? Our chicken biryani guide compares dum, fry piece and boneless biryani.

Vegetarians at the table?

Ours is a mixed kitchen serving vegetarian and non-vegetarian food. Vegetarian guests can share starters such as Golinchina Mushroom, Chitti Garelu or Paneer Kurkure. See our vegetarian Telugu food guide for more ideas.
