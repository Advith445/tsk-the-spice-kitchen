Family Lunch and Dinner in Kokapet: A Guide to Ordering at TSK

Join us at TSK The Spice Kitchen for family lunch and dinner in Kokapet, Hyderabad. Explore vegetarian dishes, chicken biryani and online ordering.

A selection of our biryanis, starters, noodles and sides.

Planning a family lunch or dinner in Kokapet? At TSK The Spice Kitchen, we serve vegetarian curries, chicken dishes and biryanis in our air-conditioned dining room on Gandipet Main Road, Hyderabad. Start with what your family enjoys, then choose starters and sides to go with your main course.

Whether you are planning a weekend lunch or getting together for dinner, here are a few ways to put your order together. Browse our current menu as you plan. Follow the dish links below to explore the options before choosing your meal.

Start with the main course

Before choosing starters, decide whether your table wants biryani, curries with rice or breads, or a mix of both. This gives the meal a starting point and helps you avoid ordering several dishes that serve the same purpose.

For a group with different preferences, plan the vegetarian main course alongside the non-vegetarian choices. That way, everyone has a meal to look forward to, rather than relying on shared starters alone.

Vegetarian food for a family meal in Kokapet

If you enjoy brinjal, consider Gutti Vankaya Kura, a stuffed-brinjal curry. Our version uses a peanut spice paste and gravy.

Kaju Tomato Curry is another option, with cashews cooked in a tomato-based gravy. Choose the curry first, then decide on rice or breads according to what your family prefers.

For a starter, Crispy Corn With Chilli Pepper brings together fried corn, chilli, pepper and garlic. Keep the rest of your order in mind when choosing how many starters to share.

We serve both vegetarian and non-vegetarian food. If your family has particular ingredient or preparation requirements, please speak with us before ordering.

Choose your chicken biryani style

We offer Chicken Dum Biryani with bone-in chicken and TSK Boneless Biryani with boneless chicken. If you already have a preference between the two, use that to narrow your choice.

Before deciding on quantities, check the available sizes and what comes with your order. If some people are also having a curry and breads, explain that when asking for a portion recommendation.

Prefer a meal with breads?

A curry-and-bread order gives people another way to build their plates. Chicken Tikka Masala, made with chicken tikka in a tomato and butter masala, is one option. Butter Naan, baked in a tandoor and brushed with butter, is one of our bread choices.

For a table ordering both vegetarian and chicken curries, choose the breads around the number of people eating each dish. These are meal-planning suggestions, rather than a set package or a fixed serving recommendation.

Order quantities around your group

Tell us how many adults and children are eating, which dishes you plan to share and whether everyone wants a full main course. Those details are more useful than guest count alone.

A biryani size label does not establish how many people it will feed. Starters, sides and appetites all affect the quantity you need, so confirm portions for your particular order.

Before You Visit Us in Kokapet

Quick answers about family dining, table bookings and online orders.

What time are you open for lunch and dinner?

We're open daily from 11 AM to 11 PM for lunch and dinner. If you're planning a larger gathering, get in touch with us about your preferred arrival time.

Do you have vegetarian options for a family meal?

Yes. We offer vegetarian dishes such as Gutti Vankaya Kura, Kaju Tomato Curry and Crispy Corn With Chilli Pepper. We also serve non-vegetarian food, so ours is a mixed kitchen.

How do I reserve a table and get the 20% booking offer?

Use the table-request section on our website and send your request on WhatsApp. Table bookings receive 20% off the total bill, with no minimum order. Your booking is confirmed only when we reply.

How do I arrange a table for 10 or more guests?

Call us on +91 99490 51777 to discuss a group visit instead of using the online table-request form. Share your date, preferred time and guest count so we can check seating availability.

Can I order delivery through Swiggy or directly from you?

Yes. You can order from our Kokapet outlet on Swiggy; enter your delivery address to check available dishes and delivery options. For direct delivery to your location, call us on +91 99490 51777 to arrange your order and confirm availability.

Can I choose the spice level for my meal?

Tell us whether you prefer mild, medium or spicy food before ordering. We'll discuss the available options for your chosen dishes so you can choose a meal that suits your taste.
